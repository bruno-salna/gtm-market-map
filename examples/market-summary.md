Of the 210 removed by size, 160 were under 10 employees and 50 had no filed accounts.

Known gaps:

## Data completeness (in-market rows)
- Domain found: 81% (high 64%, medium 12%, low 5%)
- Headcount known: 96%
- Revenue known: 94%

## Decisions made
- Rows with no industry code (2% of the register) are treated as out of the market and listed as a gap.
- Companies with no filed accounts are excluded by the size filter, and reported separately.

## Caveats
- Financial figures are from 2024; some companies will have grown or shrunk since.
- The official statistics count enterprises, not legal entities, so groups with several companies can make coverage look slightly lower than it is.
